## Q1 What should a patient do if they miss a dose of Veloxitab?
If a dose of Veloxitab is missed, it should be taken as soon as the patient remembers on the same day. If it is already the next day, the missed dose should be skipped and the next dose taken at the usual time. Patients should not take a double dose to make up for a forgotten tablet.

## Q2 Can Veloxitab tablets be crushed, split or chewed?
Veloxitab 20 mg tablets have a score line and can be split into equal halves to aid swallowing. Veloxitab 10 mg tablets have no score line and should not be split. The film coating protects the tablet from moisture; crushing or chewing has not been studied and is not recommended. Tablets should be swallowed whole with a glass of water.

## Q3 Does Veloxitab contain lactose, gluten or animal-derived ingredients?
Each Veloxitab tablet contains 45 mg lactose monohydrate. Veloxitab tablets do not contain gluten. Veloxitab tablets do not contain gelatin or other animal-derived ingredients apart from lactose, which is derived from milk. The tablets are suitable for vegetarians.

## Q4 Can Veloxitab be taken with grapefruit juice?
Grapefruit juice moderately increases the amount of velorastat in the blood. A normal glass of grapefruit juice is not expected to be a problem, but large quantities (more than one litre per day) should be avoided while taking Veloxitab.

## Q5 How should Veloxitab be disposed of?
Unused or expired Veloxitab tablets should be returned to a pharmacy for safe disposal. They should not be thrown away in household waste or flushed down the toilet.
