## Q1 How should Corvanex be stored when travelling?
Before first use, Corvanex pens should be kept in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C. When travelling, an in-use pen can be kept at room temperature below 25°C for up to 14 days. Pens should not be placed in checked-in luggage on a flight, because the hold can get very cold and the pen must not freeze. A cool bag can be used, but the pen should not touch ice packs directly.

## Q2 What should a patient do if they miss a weekly dose of Corvanex?
If a weekly dose of Corvanex is missed, it should be injected as soon as possible within 3 days (72 hours) of the missed dose. If more than 3 days have passed, the missed dose should be skipped and the next dose injected on the usual day. Two doses should not be given within 3 days of each other.

## Q3 Can the day of the weekly Corvanex injection be changed?
The day of the weekly injection can be changed if needed, as long as there are at least 3 days (72 hours) between two doses. After choosing a new day, the patient should continue with once-weekly dosing on that day.

## Q4 How should used Corvanex pens and needles be disposed of?
Used needles should be removed after each injection and placed in a sharps bin. Used pens should be disposed of according to local requirements, usually through a pharmacy or local authority sharps collection service. Pens should not be stored with a needle attached.

## Q5 What does the Corvanex pen look like and how many doses does it contain?
Each Corvanex pre-filled pen is a single-dose pen that delivers one 1 mg dose. The solution should be clear and colourless; it should not be used if it is cloudy, discoloured or contains particles.
