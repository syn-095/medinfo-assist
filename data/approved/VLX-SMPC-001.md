## 1 Name of the medicinal product
Veloxitab 10 mg film-coated tablets. Veloxitab 20 mg film-coated tablets. The active substance is velorastat, a selective angiotensin receptor modulator.

## 2 Qualitative and quantitative composition
Each 10 mg tablet contains 10 mg velorastat. Each 20 mg tablet contains 20 mg velorastat. Excipients with known effect: each tablet contains 45 mg lactose monohydrate. The tablets do not contain gluten or gelatin.

## 4.1 Therapeutic indications
Veloxitab is indicated for the treatment of essential hypertension in adults aged 18 years and over. Veloxitab is not indicated for any other condition.

## 4.2 Posology and method of administration
The recommended starting dose is 10 mg once daily. If blood pressure is not adequately controlled after 4 weeks, the dose may be increased to 20 mg once daily. The maximum dose is 20 mg once daily. Tablets may be taken with or without food, preferably at the same time each day. Patients aged 65 to 79 years should start on 10 mg once daily and be titrated with caution. Experience in patients aged 80 years and over is limited and no dosing recommendation can be made. In patients with moderate renal impairment (eGFR 30-59 mL/min) the starting dose is 10 mg and the maximum dose is 10 mg once daily. Veloxitab is not recommended in severe renal impairment (eGFR below 30 mL/min). The safety and efficacy of Veloxitab in children and adolescents under 18 years have not been established and no data are available. If a dose is missed, it should be taken as soon as remembered on the same day; a double dose should not be taken to make up for a missed dose.

## 4.3 Contraindications
Hypersensitivity to velorastat or to any of the excipients. Severe hepatic impairment. Second and third trimesters of pregnancy. Concomitant use with aliskiren in patients with diabetes mellitus. History of angioedema related to previous angiotensin receptor therapy.

## 4.4 Special warnings and precautions for use
Symptomatic hypotension may occur, particularly in volume-depleted patients or those on high-dose diuretics. Serum potassium and renal function should be monitored periodically, especially in elderly patients and patients with renal impairment. Patients with rare hereditary problems of galactose intolerance, total lactase deficiency or glucose-galactose malabsorption should not take this medicine because it contains lactose.

## 4.5 Interaction with other medicinal products and other forms of interaction
Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (for example ketoconazole, itraconazole and clarithromycin) increase velorastat exposure about two-fold; the dose should not exceed 10 mg once daily during concomitant use. Grapefruit juice moderately increases velorastat exposure and large quantities (more than one litre per day) should be avoided. Potassium supplements, potassium-sparing diuretics (for example spironolactone) and salt substitutes containing potassium may increase serum potassium; monitoring is recommended. Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) such as ibuprofen and naproxen may reduce the antihypertensive effect and increase the risk of renal impairment; monitoring of renal function is recommended. No clinically relevant interaction was seen with paracetamol, metformin, simvastatin or amlodipine. Alcohol may enhance the blood pressure lowering effect.

## 4.6 Fertility, pregnancy and lactation
There are insufficient data on the use of Veloxitab in pregnant women. Use in the first trimester is not recommended and use in the second and third trimesters is contraindicated. It is not known whether velorastat is excreted in human breast milk; there are insufficient data. Pregnant or breastfeeding women, and women planning a pregnancy, should consult their healthcare professional.

## 4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines
Dizziness or tiredness may occasionally occur, particularly at the start of treatment. Patients should not drive or use machines if affected.

## 4.8 Undesirable effects
Common side effects (may affect up to 1 in 10 people): dizziness, headache, fatigue and hyperkalaemia. Uncommon side effects (may affect up to 1 in 100 people): symptomatic hypotension, rash, pruritus, nausea and dry cough. Rare side effects (may affect up to 1 in 1,000 people): angioedema and increased liver enzymes. Suspected adverse reactions should be reported to the marketing authorisation holder's pharmacovigilance department.

## 4.9 Overdose
Limited data are available on overdose. The most likely manifestations are hypotension and tachycardia. Treatment should be symptomatic and supportive. Velorastat is not removed by haemodialysis.

## 6.3 Shelf life
3 years.

## 6.4 Special precautions for storage
Store below 30°C. Store in the original package to protect from moisture. Keep out of the sight and reach of children.
